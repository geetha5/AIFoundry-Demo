Engineering Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the platform team. Your first week priorities are:
1. Get access to the internal developer portal and request VPN credentials.
2. Set up your local dev environment using the bootstrap script in tools/setup.sh.
3. Read the incident response runbook before your on-call rotation.
4. Join the #platform-eng and #ai-gateway Slack channels.

All production deployments go through the CI/CD pipeline. Direct pushes to
production environments are disabled; every change must pass automated
evaluation and receive manual approval before promotion.

On-call engineers are expected to acknowledge pages within 10 minutes and
follow the escalation policy documented in the runbook.
